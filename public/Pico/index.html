Why Pico exists

Gaming hardware should have an affordable starting point.

Low-end on purpose

Pico is not pretending to be a flagship. The goal is to make the entry-level product good at the job it is actually built for.

Performance per dollar first

The project will care about real gaming value, power efficiency, and usable performance more than giant numbers on a spec sheet.

No fake benchmark claims

Until real silicon exists and is tested, performance numbers stay ideas rather than promises.

Easy to understand

Pico should feel simple: affordable, chill, gaming-focused, and easy to place in the lineup.

Later

Pico does not define the ceiling.

A future high-end Placeholder gaming CPU could be wildly more expensive and much larger. Ideas like 128 cores, thousands of dollars, or even ridiculous experimental core counts belong in future research — not in Pico's current spec sheet.

Future prices and core counts are concepts only. They are not announced products or guaranteed specifications.
Placeholder Pico is the chill one. Bigger ideas can come later.
